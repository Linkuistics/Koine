# Application metadata source and root accounting

## Problem

Application population counts alone cannot establish an owned-budget construction.
Repeated native slots, type-tree members, bridges, preferences, expanded path
visits and failure cleanup perform work before deduplication or publication.
Fixed per-source coefficients hide that work and cannot certify the useful subject.

## Solution

The discovery adapter produces a receipt sequence for each application job.
Receipts distinguish explicit invocations, borrowed observations, opaque native
acquisitions, owned allocation capacities and every performed byte write/read/copy.
Unknown capacities or source dimensions keep the corresponding total unknown.
The [operation and payload grammar](../verification/application-metadata-source-root-certificate.md)
defines the observable accounting boundary and its executable pure controls.

[Source accounting → Preserve raw-slot and payload receipts](../design/architecture/index.html#diagram-native-metadata-source-root)
shows the job composition. Graphs enter at their dark **Start here** node;
numbered edges give work order. Source choices, category usefulness and grants
remain governed by [workspace search](workspace-search.md#application-sources-and-native-facts),
the [reported-facts decision](../adr/search-native-facts-are-reported-observations.md)
and the [filesystem decision](../adr/filesystem-access-is-descriptor-relative.md).

## Source snapshots and receipt ownership

One job observes its explicit preference snapshot, its frozen indexed gathering,
and its running snapshot once each. Healthy empty preferences create an explicit
empty preference array. An unavailable or refused snapshot establishes no empty
inventory and triggers no implicit main-application preference selection.
Every raw item and type-tree member is inspected and charged before classification,
comparison or deduplication. Coherent seed identifiers are expanded once each;
registration output adds no recursive seed identifiers.

Each native acquisition admits all possible output cleanup first. A registration
call reserves both array and error retirement. Source batches are retired before
acquiring the next batch. Borrowed children remain valid only while their owner
is retained; converting or staging their value creates separately charged owned
work. Bridges, ARC retention, vector population and construction-failure prefixes
are explicit obligations even when native object footprints remain opaque.

## Independent root ownership

[Root ownership → Transfer once and recheck independently](../design/architecture/index.html#diagram-native-metadata-two-walks)
shows the two observations around metadata. The first component-relative walk
opens and checks the terminal directory, compares named and held identity,
transfers its existing close credit and closes its parent. A current-directory
ending transfers the already-held directory after its status observation; a final
parent ending opens and checks that parent. Every expanded visit, link/dot/error
branch and actual local construction has a receipt.

The first finalized resolved spelling determines ordinary metadata admission.
At most 1,023 lossless UTF-8 bytes permits the root resource-type observation;
only positive checked application status admits the fixed reader. Longer roots
require actual indexed facts and perform no root-resource or ordinary-reader
call. Metadata owners retire before the first root closes. A fresh second walk
must establish a directory with equal device/inode; it retains the first finalized
spelling. Failed/replaced identity refuses the copy. No third walk or registration
re-walk per copy is part of this interface. Separate observations remain race-prone.

## Exhaustion and cancellation

Call, count, cumulative gross or deadline exhaustion freezes optional work and
publication as FAILED/null. A pending invocation retains its inputs, all possible
output cleanup and the shared slot until actual return. Cleanup spends its prior
reservation; disposal and reuse refund no performed byte or attempt. Individual
metadata refusal follows the selected reader policy without a convenient fallback.

## Test seams and construction limit

### Apply the selected explicit-work boundary at composition

The [accounting ADR](../adr/application-metadata-accounting-covers-explicit-work.md)
selects controlled raw requested capacities and performed raw writes, plus every
explicit adapter/local/native operation and cleanup. Internal platform/Swift
constructors, bridges/COW, metadata, allocator and compiler ownership traffic are
excluded, with no total heap/RSS/peak promise. Explicit caller work is still
charged. ProviderValue publication remains provider-owned. These exclusions are
not measured zero costs or unknown explicit operands.

The [runtime contract](application-metadata-runtime-admission.md) specifies
real native/Swift access, raw staging/owner/growth/field recipes and the real
public-value adapter. The [owned composition](../verification/application-metadata-composition.md)
retains the strict failure as comparison and derives complete scoped expressions.
Actual source tuples, expanded visits, preferences, final values and errors remain
conditional; useful fit is not established. Unknown explicit operands prevent
optional calls; excluded internals require no strict runtime certificate. Reserve
all charged work and retained/possible cleanup, including raw state writes, before
calls. Keep all performed failure prefixes and the slot until true return.

The complete selected wire is independent; its post-encoding cap funds no earlier
allocation. All gates, source/category/locale breadth, authority/action/Settings/
disclosure/compatibility and native acceptance obligations remain. The family
consumption hold and whole-family reconciliation/review remain; no new quota,
ABI, support/posture change or useful loss follows.

The [owned-storage subcertificate](../verification/application-metadata-owned-storage.md)
specifies requested raw-buffer capacity separately from performed initialization,
input, projection and growth writes. Two immutable inputs and bounded conversion
scratch overlap with explicitly retained parser owners; reuse never refunds gross.
Its per-episode known local terms preserve all saved aliases/raw keys and outside
paths. They remain distinct from actual Swift/CF bridge layouts, expanded-walk
work, final result conversion and the independent complete response certificate.

The human-confirmed seams remain public GraphQL, actual provider/host binaries
and isolated signed supported macOS VMs. Pure receipt controls exercise duplicate
slots, empty/refused preferences, native return shapes, expanded walks, ownership,
replacement, long-root refusal and every cancellation prefix. They establish
neither actual source payloads nor native usefulness, serviceability or peak memory.

The scoped representation/scratch/publication design is specified; numeric
whole-job fit and the actual final response remain conditional.
The [selected-wire contract](../verification/application-metadata-wire.md) derives
complete finite full-field selections, response-key merging, independent root
aliases, JSON escapes and failure/error envelopes. Unknown final values keep
actual wire unknown. The core checks the complete encoded body against its
independent response cap; replacement with null data establishes no pre-encoding
owned allocation or peak bound. Final scalar/publication receipts use the selected explicit-work scope,
separately from wire bytes; excluded runtime backing is disclosed.
The [certificate handoff](../verification/application-metadata-source-root-certificate.md#handoff-and-failed-construction)
names its unresolved design terms separately from already pending native evidence.
The application 20 MiB cumulative gross, 4,194,304 attempts, independent core
8 MiB response, three/five-second deadlines and four true-return slots bind.
The whole-family consumption hold and mandatory whole-producer review remain.
This spec changes no gate, source/category, locale quota, posture or action contract.
