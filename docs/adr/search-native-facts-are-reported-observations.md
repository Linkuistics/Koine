# Search native facts are reported observations

Search publishes checked native-service observations rather than proof of the
platform's internal health. UNREGISTERED means the checked registration result
reported no entry for the present native bundle identity; the
[observation contract](../specs/workspace-search.md#native-application-and-file-metadata)
permits only its exact not-found or completely checked nonmatching-array branches,
without a conflicting preferred observation. Nil identifier remains UNKNOWN
with affecting coverage and never asserts a missing identifier. Type/package/
hidden/alias facts and positive identifiers likewise report native values.

An undetected internal failure or stale service state can produce an incorrect
fact, omit a name or misclassify a choice. This is surprising and costly to
reverse once clients depend on registration states and chooser behavior.
Malformed, incomplete, blocked or conflicting observations remain uncertain;
observed resource errors refuse negative finalization. Known incorrect witnesses
and UNKNOWN metadata for every configured launcher still fail acceptance.
Actions retain their existing revalidation and authority contracts.

Proving every internal acquisition healthy was the rejected stronger fact
meaning: the documented public APIs expose no such health token, and the
[source/SDK analysis](../verification/search-native-resources.md#call-health-is-separate-from-descriptor-visibility)
does not supply it. Positive-only registration or replacing native services with
suffixes/private registration scraping would lose required launch semantics.
Revisit if a supported health-bearing native route becomes available under the
existing posture, or measured incorrect facts defeat useful configured launch
coverage. This decision provides no opaque-allocation bound; the independently
selected [empirical evidence contract](../specs/workspace-search.md#test-seams-and-acceptance)
has its own limitations. Complete-resource review and native acceptance remain
required.
