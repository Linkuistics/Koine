## Findings

The scope keeps every missing return unavailable, and no path turns a gap into
execution authority. The Fe bound, the raw retention terms, the local work and
the inventories match the accepted boundary. The six findings below name the
location, the consequence and the smallest correction. Findings 1 to 4 are
medium. Findings 5 and 6 are low. None adopts a capacity, a count or an
authority.

Line numbers refer to `KEEPALIVE-EVIDENCE-SCOPE.md` unless a finding
names another file.

- Finding 1, medium. The category checklist has no row for input occurrences
  or for the OCR cycle count. Line 58 makes the categories at lines 70 to 79 a
  coverage checklist. KEEPALIVE-ACCOUNTING.md lines 85 to 87 charge each input
  occurrence with its internal connection and `confirm_input_read` work. They
  also charge key and modifier events, scroll decomposition and their sleeps.
  Line 86 of that file says the 1×1 update request does not bound received
  rectangles or frame allocations. Accounting lines 88 to 91 charge every
  capture, encode and recognition cycle in an OCR operation. That count and its
  wire and native costs must come from the dependency's unit schema. Accounting
  line 588 puts input-update and OCR-cycle bounds in the dependency row that
  k125 scopes. The OCR row at line 78 names the claims of one recognition only.
  Line 92 requires the study to preserve poll and input order. It does not
  bound them. Lines 62 to 66 do require every actual unit. A return that is
  complete against the checklist can still omit both bounds, and nothing makes
  the omission visible. K126 then has no bound for any input occurrence or OCR
  operation in the caller workload. Add a checklist row for input-occurrence
  work. Add the maximum cycle count per operation to the OCR row. Cite
  accounting lines 85 to 91 and 588.

- Finding 2, medium. The recognition count is a plan, not a maximum derived
  from the polling policy. Lines 164 to 175 plan three recognition calls, one
  each for V-I-fit, V-R-fit and V-R-excess. The contract keeps the request's
  OCR engine and unchanged polling semantics
  (`settings-capture-resource-contract.md` lines 38 and 114). The survey
  records that OCR polling checks elapsed time only after recognition returns
  (`docs/research/settings-keepalive-enforcement-a.md` lines 261 to 266).
  Accounting line 89 charges every internal recognition cycle. One producer
  operation can therefore call Vision more than once in its 10-second
  interval. This can occur when the first cycle does not match the fixture text.
  Lines 177 to 181 forbid the study to repeat Vision. The producer's own loop
  is not the study's choice. Before any execution request, derive the maximum
  calls per operation from the pinned polling source: stop condition, period
  and deadline. Request that maximum, or pin a unit that limits recognition to
  one cycle. Record a call above the plan as a failed case, not as extra data.
  Also name each case's frame source. Line 166 states that the study starts no VM. A
  fixture that enters through a protocol peer adds a process, wire bytes and a
  role outside the four roles at line 205. A fixture that enters through a
  direct image entry needs its own source correspondence.

- Finding 3, medium. R3 can be requested when its fixed burst cannot block.
  Lines 236 to 240 give each R3 writer one fixed 8192-byte burst during the
  pre-read hold. KEEPALIVE-RAW-CONTROLS.md line 119 fixes that count. A write
  blocks only when the stream's accepted capacity is below its burst. That
  capacity is part of `Q`, which lines 211 to 217 leave unfilled. No recorded
  figure shows a capacity below 8192 bytes. The only recorded figure is the
  withdrawn 64 KiB constant that lines 211 to 217 reject. RAW-CONTROLS lines
  127 to 129 and line 366 say that an unblocked schedule leaves the
  blocked-writer obligation open. Lines 253 to 254 make R3 unavailable only for
  want of an instrument. A study can therefore be authorised and run with a
  predictable unblocked result. Make the R3 request conditional on the `Q`
  return. If the returned capacity of either route is at least the burst, mark
  R3 infeasible as designed. Route the schedule change to k126 or to a new
  design chain, because the accepted boundary fixes the 8192-byte count. Do not change the
  count in the integration.

- Finding 4, medium. The scope does not name crash reporting, which the
  expected loader failure triggers. Lines 226 to 234 design a case whose
  expected outcome is a dynamic-loader abort for a missing library. The
  contract notes that native work can also end in a crash or signal
  (`settings-capture-resource-contract.md` lines 91 to 92). On macOS, the
  crash-reporting service normally records such terminations, including
  loader failures at process start. It writes the report outside the private root, in
  the user's diagnostic-report store. The study host runs that service. Each
  preflight process listing shows `ReportCrash` agent and daemon processes,
  for example
  `docs/verification/settings-activation/deferred-01/deferred-preflight.json`.
  No accepted boundary document and no part of the scope names this effect.
  Line 43 requires all children and services. Line 205 leaves services
  unbounded. Neither names the known effect of the case that predicts it. The
  report is a host write outside the root, a service in process overlap and a
  record that can name private-root paths. Removal would be cleanup work that
  no plan budgets (lines 296 to 302). A manifest written from the scope can
  omit the effect and still look complete. Name crash reporting as a known
  effect of the loader case and of native failure. Require the evidence owner
  to establish its behaviour on the pinned host. Then bound and retain the
  report under the storage and privacy rules, or establish a declared
  suppression, or leave the case unavailable.

- Finding 5, low. The diagram and one sentence misstate the scope's state.
  `keepalive-evidence.mmd` has four scope branches: producer, platform, raw and
  local. The native OCR proposal at lines 162 to 207 has no node. Every path
  to K126 passes through the authorised study node. Lines 346 to 354 also hand
  k126 inputs that need no execution: the separate inventories, the
  `856 + 168u` carrier size and the successor and final sequences. The hold
  node is a dead end, but lines 331 to 335 route unobtainable bounds to a
  human choice. Line 110 calls the Fe route "selected", while line 113 calls it
  a method to review that is currently unavailable. Add the native branch. Add
  an edge from the manifest to K126 for the inputs that need no execution. Add
  an edge from hold to the human choice. Replace "selected" with "proposed" at
  line 110.

- Finding 6, low. The document uses semicolons in 30 lines, for example lines
  25, 46, 51, 74 and 76. It carries no departure marker. The session's
  controlled-English rule forbids semicolons. K138's finding 9 raised the same
  point for `KEEPALIVE-CALLER.md`. Convert the prose, or mark justified
  departures in table cells.

## Checked and clean

- The Fe bound at lines 115 to 136 has the right direction. The post-exec
  sample `S` equals Fe minus removals plus additions. Additions are not
  negative, so Fe is at most `S + R`, and `E` covers metric and observer error.
  Lines 104 to 108 keep Fi separate and use the larger of Fi and Fe. Lines 146
  to 151 refuse an L3 substitute and assume no debugger entitlement. Lines 153
  to 160 keep the bootstrap, backend, collector, loader and service intervals
  separate. Setter correspondence, the removal audit and perturbation precede
  use, and a change invalidates them. Nothing claims Fe as proved.
- The native slice has four operations. Four 10-second intervals plus
  20-second containment give 120 seconds, under the 600-second ceiling. Two raw
  objects of 256 KiB per operation give 2 MiB, under the 8 MiB ceiling. The
  authorised Vision count stays zero (line 168).
- Missing subjects cannot become authority. Line 7, the admission gate at
  lines 48 to 52 and line 207 keep missing returns unavailable and request no
  permission.
- `Q` covers every pipe route. Retention of `Lout + Lerr + 1 + Q`, the two
  4096-byte buffers and the 16 KiB control match RAW-CONTROLS lines 98 and 324
  to 333. The loader subject stays a candidate, as RAW-CONTROLS lines 138 to
  142 require. Lines 236 to 259 keep release without waiting and the
  prefix-plus-remainder oracle. They keep exact R5 semantics at 1024 bytes,
  the separate audit of both copy sites and the handling-only scope.
- The host work table covers archive, transcript and recovery scan. It covers
  local-audit indices 15 to 21 and 24 to 25 (KEEPALIVE-CALLER.md line 101).
  It also covers blocked filesystem and process work. Lines 296 to 302 make the six-process ceiling
  inapplicable, block on unknown holders and reset no containment window for
  an item or a cleanup. Lines 304 to 314 keep the `spctl` enabled and disabled
  states and make unknown and typed not-found results unavailable. Start
  stages, inventory, identity, `U_start` and the 600-second queue term match
  accounting line 146 and KEEPALIVE-CALLER.md line 144.
- Lines 348 to 354 keep separate inventories. They cover the 13 mechanism
  cases, the 17 R cases, the loader case and the 18 S2–S6 cases. They also
  cover the record and recovery controls, producer units, Fe and native cases. The carrier and sequence numbers match k119 and k124. K126
  keeps the full fit, and the review of the whole owner stays separate.
- KEEPALIVE-QUALIFICATION.md lines 28 to 31 and the README restate the scope
  without enlarging the 13 cases.
