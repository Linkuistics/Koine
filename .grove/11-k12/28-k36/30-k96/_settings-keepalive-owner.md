# settings-keepalive-owner-k96 — brief

## Goal

Design a useful serialised keepalive command owner with enforceable finite
resources. It preserves capture qualification, first failure and original ordinary
input semantics. This level earns its place through a distinct owner subsystem,
new lifecycle/scheduling/producer/storage/recovery context and a joint complete
reviewed design needed by its consumers.

## Done when

Complete every original-duty row, the useful numerical workload/profile,
cooperative-stop and containment contracts and meaningful coordinator controls.
Then obtain independent whole-design review and every earned integration before
externalised planning, implementation and earned review/integration. Only that
completed sequence permits k89/k81/k76 adoption. Partial design reviews and source
controls do not satisfy this joint handoff. Do not pre-cut implementation work
before the remaining design settles. This is ordinary ordered design work, not a
pass series or an independently accepted product increment.

## Accepted contract

One serialised owner governs ticks, ordinary commands, validation, publication,
failure and cleanup in one lock/latch/ordinal domain. Only logged UI-independent
waits and deferred ticks allowed by the agreement change elapsed timing.
Preserve input order, sleeps/polls and one attempt. Required capture validation and first
failure precede later input.

A planned cooperative worker stop has a separate
matched lifecycle record and confirmed exit, and cannot erase earlier failures.
Unexpected exit, interrupted required capture or missing stop evidence remains
unavailable. Retain finite enforceable complete originals and partials, with
protected final/cleanup capacity. Unqualified holder/storage boundaries hold.
