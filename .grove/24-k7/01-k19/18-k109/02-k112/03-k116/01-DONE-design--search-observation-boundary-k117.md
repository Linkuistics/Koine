## Decisions (running log)

- Separate the platform's documented value/error observation from a proof of
  its internal resource health. The latter is not supplied by CFError, nil,
  successful later metadata checks, or current graph/source evidence.
- Recommend native reported observations and controlled independent outcome
  acceptance with explicit opaque-visibility limits for human consideration.
  This weaker boundary is unapproved and cannot clear original k116/k109 gates.
- Specify existing native metadata/Bundle/type services and the error-bearing
  LS all-registration query plus separate preferred observation. Nil identifier
  is always UNKNOWN with affecting coverage. A normal-looking hidden-error
  negative is the precise residual semantics decision, not a passed health test.
- Native call envelopes and complete owned/hook events leave opaque internal
  acquisitions unproved. The proposed empirical acceptance still fails observed
  independent resource errors/backoff; it is a separately unapproved evidence
  change, never a complete observer claim.
- No ADR/glossary change is earned yet: no durable runtime/public semantics
  decision is selected. Existing coverage/identity/traversal/authority ADRs and
  terms remain binding. SDL/examples retain current field meanings; only their
  proposal-status comments change. k118 owns answers, k119 final reconciliation.
