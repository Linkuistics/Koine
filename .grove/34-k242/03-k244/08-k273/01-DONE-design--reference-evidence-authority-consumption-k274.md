## Decisions (running log)

Use one immutable permit receipt whose fixed unspent name is atomically renamed to a
fixed spent name in the same held authority directory, before creating original attempt
evidence or releasing contact. An unchanged receipt cannot be reused from the spent name;
missing/uncheckable unspent authority refuses. Attempt/export deletion never reconstructs
that name. Compare with attempt-only exclusive creation (R2's known defect) and a mutable
receipt-state overwrite (torn-format recovery burden); rename keeps permit bytes immutable
and gives one explicit cooperative consumption point. Namespace/full-synchronization
support still requires actual scratch acknowledgements and the selected bounded assumption.

Reserve and validate the caller's exclusive export before consumption; retain failed
preflight evidence without automatic cleanup. After consumption, every error/death
remains consumed even before source contact. Preserve original attempt segments at the
fixed subject, then independently verify any complete or explicitly incomplete derivative
against those originals. No failed export, moved attempt, lock release or anchor correction
refunds authority. Full trust/contact/entry design remains the later children's work.

Retain an exclusive immutable consume.intent authority event before the permit rename.
Its partial presence blocks fresh admission if death or an ambiguous rename leaves the
unspent receipt unchanged. It belongs beside the permit rather than inside disposable
attempt/export evidence. No failed transition removes that event or reconstructs unspent
authority. This supplies conservative uncertainty handling without a reusable qualified
flag or an inferred second initialization.
