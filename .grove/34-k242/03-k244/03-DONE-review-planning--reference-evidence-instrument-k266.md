**F1 — The one-pass association is not realizable as specified (high).**
Coordinates: k264 step 2; k263 brief Notes; contract lines 266–278; k249 decision
log. The plan says the pass is bound to its attempt and that "any prior
acquisition-start evidence for that permission" is checked, with the evidence kept
"in the attempt records, without adding a session-status file". The attempt records
live only in the caller-chosen fresh destination, so a second attempt that names a
different fresh output has no path to the first attempt's header. The plan never says
where prior-start evidence is discovered (a derived location, a scanned root, an
exclusive-create marker), so "any unestablished prior-attempt check refuses" has no
defined subject, and the k265 step 4 test ("a previously started permission presented
with a different fresh output") has no defined scratch arrangement. Output that was
deleted, moved or placed on another volume is undetectable by any record-only scheme.
Impact: output exclusivity alone is exactly what the k249 decision said is
insufficient; a second fresh name can spend a second pass. Contract basis: contract
lines 276–278; k244 brief Done when 4; k245 notes ("no silent repeat").

**F2 — Reconciliation edits follow the measurement they would invalidate, and the
qualified identity set is never partitioned from reporting documents (high).**
Coordinates: k265 steps 2, 3, 5, 7; k264 step 2; k244 brief Context. Step 3 pins
"permission/configuration/Taskfile" and every measurement input; step 5 then edits the
contract, "canonical population summary" and the D2/SVG views; step 7 writes the proof
identity into k242/k245. The permission is written only in the canonical population
document (k244 brief Context), and the repository's own freeze convention in the k248
and k249 Evidence digests every Markdown/JSON/D2/SVG. The plan does not say which
artifact constitutes the "frozen permission" identity or which files are inside the
qualified set. If the document is inside it, step 5 invalidates the run and packaging's
"matched permission/input identities" refuse at k245; if the permission is a constant in
the tool, the plan must say so and say how its equality with the document is
established. The proof inventory also cannot be written into a document that is itself
pinned. Contract basis: contract lines 500–509 and k244 brief Done when 3 and 5.

**F3 — The admission proof interface is consumed before it is produced, and the

**F9 — Decoder qualification claims independence for facts that are runtime
observations (medium).**
Coordinates: k254 steps 1, 4, 5 and Done when; k253 brief Done when; k252 Context.

independent oracle result. Contract basis: contract lines 200–209 and 478–481.
