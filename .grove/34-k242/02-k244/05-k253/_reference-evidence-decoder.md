Provide a reproducible in-process Core Foundation byte decoder and complete
metadata witness export, independently checked on authored original payloads.

qualified on independent pure specimens or remain exact live qualification gaps.

actual proof. No conversion or unsupported value becomes complete typed evidence.

The earlier offline replay checks continue to pass at both handoffs.
