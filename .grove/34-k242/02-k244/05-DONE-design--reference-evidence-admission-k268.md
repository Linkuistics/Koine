  If the observer cannot be qualified within authorized pure scope, retain that

- F9: Distinguish independent byte-format/type/position expectations, independently
  acquired pinned runtime observations and regression evidence learned from output.

## Decisions (running log)

F2/F3 — Separate the immutable permission profile, canonical requirement extract,
qualified executable/configuration/fixtures and independently frozen applicability
inventory from proof/report outputs. Admission recomputes proofs against the required
inventory, never against a proof's own item list. Reconciliation precedes measurement;
promotion references proofs only from documents outside the measured read set.

F9 — Byte-format/type/position expectations, independently acquired pinned CF runtime
observations and producer-learned regression expectations have different provenance.
Require a separately authored narrow native observation programme for CF acceptance,
storage, duplicate survivors and coercions; agreement qualifies the pinned observation,
not a universal serialization guarantee. Regression-only facts cannot close a required
independent coordinate.

F11 — Preserve the descriptor-relative kernel-semantics ADR and exact inherited
literal AND resolved component prefixes. /System/Volumes/Data remains a /System
spelling; no sealed-volume or /System-except-Data policy was selected. Mount/firmlink
fixtures distinguish syntactic scope from observed identity/topology; hidden redirection
and transient topology remain limits, not scope rewrites. No current topology probe.

F1 — The current permission contains neither an anchored attempt subject nor proof of
uncertain first use. Asked Antony whether the design should require separate explicit
authority to initialize a fixed permission-evidence location. Until settled and actually
recorded, absence cannot admit a target pass. No authority store or receipt is created
by this design session.

F4/F5 — Select an external Darwin DTrace syscall/process collector as an explicitly
unqualified candidate. Read its installed manual and Apple's SIP description; neither
establishes available probes, privilege or lossless coverage. Independent direct-scratch
syscall/child bypass and collector-loss controls are required before real adapter credit.
Rehearse the exact retained packaging parser/constants/coordinator/adapters and actual
Taskfile edges through a sealed scratch contact gate; distinguish requested fixed intents
from real mapped scratch calls. No real mdfind/installed-root positive or platform/posture
change is authorized. Missing external observation or realizable wiring proof stays live
before affected consumers; wrapper logs/static inspection cannot replace it.

Use the existing durable contract and D2 viewer rather than a duplicate spec/ADR or formal
model. The central missing evidence concerns real bytes, runtime/syscall observations
and crash/persistence boundaries; a checked abstract state machine could not supply it.
The cooperative reservation protocol and its reachability/counterexample controls are
stated explicitly without claiming model checking. Whole-producer review is scheduled,
so no in-session reviewer is spawned.

F1 handoff — No answer or authorization receipt was received. Externalized the concrete
initialization/reservation/cooperative-tamper trade-off as requirements
reference-evidence-authority-k270 before k245. The durable contract specifies the fixed
subject and conservative protocol conditionally; it grants no new authority. K269 owns
early proof/inventory/collector construction and later review/repair/final qualification/
close placement. Pure construction can qualify its scratch mechanism; actual packaging
remains held on the independent receipt and every actual proof. Every original k244
Done when and k245/k246/k247 duty remains live.
