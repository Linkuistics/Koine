## Decisions (running log)

Use a fixed mdfind NUL-delimited byte stream with independent stdout/stderr and
true terminal status; no text splitting, sorting/deduplication or shell query
construction. Choose an in-process Core Foundation parser/type-ID walker over
immutable bytes rather than plistlib/plutil normalization. Its exact compiled
image/runtime and complete typed/duplicate/coercion limits require qualification
on pure specimens before target contact; the source/SDK selects a route, not a pass.

The proposed envelope is koine.application-reference-evidence/1 with closed tagged
records and canonical raw-byte/UTF-16 encodings, preserving logical occurrences
separately from shared objects. Construction owns the executable exhaustive schema
and independently authored verifier. Pure qualification must exercise actual pipe
and descriptor adapters on constructed fixtures as well as injected faults; mock-only
controls cannot qualify them. No mdfind invocation or installed root enters pure mode.

K244 remains the original complete instrument obligation. Its design child does
not materialize speculative Taskfile wrappers: executable pure qualification and
output-only packaging recipes belong to construction and must be proved before k245.
K246 retains generated rewrite discrimination, separately from original-byte integrity.
