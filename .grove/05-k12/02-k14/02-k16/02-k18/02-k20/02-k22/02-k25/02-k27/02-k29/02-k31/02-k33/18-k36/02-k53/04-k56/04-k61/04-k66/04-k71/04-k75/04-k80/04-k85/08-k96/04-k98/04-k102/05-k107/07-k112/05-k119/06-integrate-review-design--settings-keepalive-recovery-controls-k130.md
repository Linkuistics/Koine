  or noise, with a reason.

  touches. Where a finding changes the case inventory, reconcile the receipt,
  the accounting table, both views and the summaries.
- Keep every approved substitution as approved. Add no collector site, allocator
  seam or production injection. Present any new controlled-result shape to
  Antony as an explicit choice with costs. Infer no approval.
- Present any loss of an original S, lifecycle or scheduling obligation to
  Antony as an explicit choice with costs. Infer no relaxation.
- Adopt no numerical capacity, queue bound, storage result or mechanism result.
  Keep every prerequisite and the complete-profile duties live in k119 and the
  parents.
- Record each disposition in the edited documents or this leaf. Check the
  edited documents and views. Retire only this leaf.
