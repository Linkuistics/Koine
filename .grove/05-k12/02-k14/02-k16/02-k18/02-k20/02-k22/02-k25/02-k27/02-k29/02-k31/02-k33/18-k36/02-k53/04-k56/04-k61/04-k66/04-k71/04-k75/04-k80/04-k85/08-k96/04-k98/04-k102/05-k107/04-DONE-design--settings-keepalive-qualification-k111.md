  protected-capacity and process-loss recovery questions. Cover collection-backed
  directories, terminal-backed global fence/bindings, original-lock recovery and
  every clean/failed/final row. Distinguish process loss from device/power loss.

  prerequisites. Include dependency availability and external ownership. Do not
  ask permission for a study that still lacks an exact subject or finite bounds.
- Produce the reviewable proposal, then cut independent review before any execution
  decision or k112 consumption. Native execution needs explicitly agreed reviewed
  scope. Do not create speculative implementation leaves or start a VM.
