### R1 — The missing evidence is posed as one archive question, but it splits into two disjoint, differently recoverable cohorts (high)

- **Contract.** The recommendation is to "assess whether a byte-complete,
  digest-matching archive of the credited capture can be supplied" and otherwise
  to seek "bounded evidence packaging or change the design-evidence requirement"
  (`shape`:71-76, 81). k240 inherits "first determine whether such an archive
  exists" (k240:25); the root brief, `acc`:348-353 and seven charters repeat it.
- **Evidence.** The cohorts share no name-file type. All 247 loctables and only
  two other name files belong to the 290 /System copies, which have no per-locale
  InfoPlist.strings; the 350 non-system copies hold 348 information files and
  1,160 per-locale strings and no loctable. All 538 /System file stamps carry one
  identical 2026-08-13 timestamp: a sealed, build-determined volume whose bytes
  are fixed by 25G83 and checkable against the saved SHA-256 from any 25G83
  system image. The development host now runs macOS 27.0.1 (26A434), so neither
  the host nor an "archive of the capture" reaches those bytes, while third-party
  copies depend on vendor versions that have probably moved. Separately, P1024
  runs on the supported VM's own baseline, which already supplies real, complete
  system apps at that VM's build (`acc`:391; `shape`:111-112). Historical /System
  tuples matter to the 1,010 test apps only if the still-unstated assignment rule
  draws templates from them.
- **Failure.** Antony is asked whether an archive exists. The likely answer is
  no, leaving "authorize packaging" or "change the evidence requirement" —
  without being told that the hardest part (every loctable and 10,794 of 13,672
  direct locale records) is recoverable and digest-verifiable from a public build
  image, or may not be needed at all. A requirement change or an over-broad
  authorization could follow from a mis-posed question.
- **Consequence.** State the gap per cohort: what is missing, its candidate
  recovery source and verifiability (build image, vendor versions, current
  host), and whether that cohort feeds the test-app shape at all. Re-pose k240's
  question on that basis.

### R2 — No document says which real population the P1024 shape must represent (medium)

- **Contract.** `qual`:349 requires the shape "against the independent measured
  real distribution or an explicit justified envelope"; `acc`:358 says "from a
  measured real distribution or justified envelope". `shape`:85-86 disclaims any
  "probability, confidence, representativeness or universal 1,024-app-machine
  claim", and `shape`:16-17 calls the census neither an exhaustive host
  population nor the supported VM baseline.
- **Evidence.** The only measured corpus is one development host at a superseded
  OS, with a distinctive inventory (96 of the 350 non-system copies sit under
  /Library/Application Support, many of them plug-in managers and uninstallers).
  The recommended route recovers exactly that corpus, but neither the boundary nor
  the spec says why that inventory is the reference rather than Antony's current
  machine or the supported VM, or how an explicit multiplicity rule
  (`shape`:96-98) turns at most 640 tuples into 1,010 apps while remaining a
  measured distribution.
- **Failure.** Byte-complete recovery of the historical corpus can succeed and the
  subject design still cannot justify its shape, because the reference was never
  chosen. The evidence question k240 asks sits downstream of one nobody has asked.
- **Consequence.** Name the reference population and the reason for it as part of
  the boundary k240 decides on.

### R3 — The alternatives table contrasts the routes on the wrong axis (medium)

- **Contract.** Alternative 2 is rejected because "helpers/copies are not a
  measured useful-app cohort" and repeated projections lack formats; the
  recommended route is credited "with independently verified useful-copy
  classification" (`shape`:81-82). Alternative 3 is a "synthetic
  maximum-size/keys/languages envelope" (`shape`:83).
- **Evidence.** Every route reuses tuples: at most 640 saved tuples stand against
  1,010 required distinct apps, and the reproduction contract itself requires "an
  explicit multiplicity/assignment rule" (`shape`:97-98). Byte recovery restores
  formats and candidates but neither classifies useful copies nor removes
  repetition, and the classification credited to it has no stated source. The
  packet already carries partial classification facts — LSUIElement or
  LSBackgroundOnly true on 179 copies, 13 nested spellings — which
  `shape`:15-16 ("location alone does not classify") omits, though they are the
  reader's own inputs and so not independent. The envelope route is evaluated
  only in its weakest, global form. A per-copy envelope that keeps every saved
  joint dimension and declares only the unsaved format/value choices is the
  reading of `qual`:349's "explicit justified envelope" the saved evidence most
  nearly supports; its real weakness, no argument that the declared choices
  dominate opaque native parse cost, is not stated.
- **Failure.** k240 is told recovery solves what it does not (classification,
  multiplicity), and the spec's own envelope route is dismissed in a straw form.
- **Consequence.** Contrast the routes on each axis separately — formats and
  candidates, classification source, multiplicity — and evaluate the per-copy
  envelope with its actual justification gap.

### R4 — The loss inventory is a hand-written list plus a counted key sweep, not an enumeration, and it misses a loss (medium)

- **Contract.** The boundary is the "exact" corpus-insufficiency handoff, and
  k240 may select "narrowly bounded evidence packaging" (k240:26-27), whose scope
  would follow the list of missing facts.
- **Evidence.** `missingEvidence` counts regular files lacking the keys
  `payload`, `format` and `typedEntries` (`audit`:84-88), names the census schema
  never used, so each count equals the file count by construction and could
  never read otherwise; the test's `formatTags == fixedFilePayloads`
  (scripts/test_application_population_metadata_shape.py:30) is the same
  identity. The conclusion is right — the 19 file-row fields are a closed set
  with no payload or serialization tag — but that follows from enumeration,
  which neither instrument performs. Enumerating `measure_file`'s projection
  field by field finds a loss absent from `missingJointFacts` (`audit`:152-159)
  and from `shape`:41-59: flags are kept only when Boolean (`census`:151-154), so
  `None` covers both an absent key and a non-Boolean value, two distinct reader
  branches (`acc`:412, "true/false/missing/malformed flags"). The packet holds 474
  such LSUIElement and 614 such LSBackgroundOnly values, which bear directly on
  helper/agent shape.
- **Failure.** A packaging scope derived from the listed facts would omit a fact
  the shape needs, and the frozen manifest records counted "evidence" that could
  not have come out any other way.
- **Consequence.** Derive missing facts by enumerating every projected field
  against the reader's required facts, with a control showing a row that carries
  the fact is counted present.
