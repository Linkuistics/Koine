# process-protocol-agreement-k87

## Goal

Deliver k75's complete feasible, explicitly agreed public-AX protocol and k52
implementation handoff after consuming capture, lifecycle and consent evidence.
All current k75 criteria apply; completed children are not a substitute for them.

## Context

Read the current capture/public Accessibility ADRs, spec/SDL/operations, client
guide and k52's entire renewal table. k104 explicitly accepted wrong-target
reads/effects, stale notifications, mistaken withdrawal and unbounded framework
AX acquisition. Do not re-ask those choices or require private authentication,
publication/loss barriers or an owned AX exchange. The capture-transfer resource
contract remains strict; k104 supplies no feasibility result for it.

## Done when

- Agree the evidenced capture/transfer/discovery/peer-authentication/grant
  protocol, acquisition bounds, lifecycle consequence, Koine-owned consent and
  local ownership/budgets. Surface remaining concrete native conflicts before
  presenting a feasible protocol; no implicit helper or client AX consent.
- Specify best-effort CURRENT/REMEMBERED rows, unanswered reads, failed
  registration, notification loss/staleness, local overflow, partial listings,
  withdrawal scopes and recovery. A platform closure report may be wrong;
  withdrawal remains irreversible. Locally obsolete callback ownership must not
  revive old references or access freed state. Do not require authenticated
  window-lifetime evidence as a condition of ordinary public AX results.
- Agree opaque grammar/non-reuse, malformed/absent/stale distinctions, finite
  allocator scope/exhaustion and bounded terminal-reason reclamation, with a
  non-time namespace across client/provider/Koine/boot restarts. Explicitly agree
  any loss of served application-reference restart survival and prepublication
  koine-desktop/1 framing. Randomness or cached PID/version is not silently a
  non-collision proof.
- Specify focus attempts, framework-reported acceptance/observations, partial
  progress, uncertainty and refusal before any native work is invoked. Expose
  wrong-process/window data/effects, stale reports, false withdrawal and framework
  receive risk in SDL descriptions and client docs. A failed public AX call may
  have acted; no exact-target success description or automatic replay.
- Reconcile spec, design SDL/operations, current ADRs, glossary, README, client
  guide and architecture views. Label served behavior until implementation. Use
  existing GraphQL/native plugin/VM seams; agree a genuinely necessary new seam.
- Complete k52's provider/schema/reference/client/test instructions and every
  evidence-renewal row for rebuilt signed/notarized bytes. Include callback-source
  migration, local withdrawal/non-revival, actual restoration where reachable,
  all restart boundaries, independent ordinary-focus witnesses, and actual PID
  recycling versus deterministic mismatch. Mark old evidence historical; do not
  test excluded exact-target or hard framework-acquisition guarantees as gates.
- Review the complete load-bearing protocol before implementation, with no
  duplicate in-session reviewer beside a scheduled review. Check k75/k72/k57/
  k54/k51 on retirement; preserve live work for each retained unmet criterion.

## Notes

The timestamp implementation is not approved for first release. k52 already owns
implementation; add no implementation leaf from this design unless planning later
establishes that decomposition is needed.
