# observation-retirement-contract-k81


## Goal

Specify the observation admission and permanent retirement construction under
k80's observed-closure agreement, and the precise native discriminator it
requires. Deliver a labelled proposal and reviewable evidence boundary; native
execution and the complete product agreement belong to later k75 children.



## Context

Consume k78, k79 and k80, the adoption synthesis, current capture/effect ADRs
and the native-targeting section of the machine spec. The served PID observer
is not evidence of the future retained-endpoint observation protocol.

## Done when

- The machine spec and visual discussion distinguish preparing, usable,
  authenticated closure retirement and withdrawal for insufficient observation.
- Publication, sends, retirement, late replies, old registrations and resource
  release have an explicit local ordering and separate native proof obligations.
- Space absence is not closure; unknown provenance produces no accepted data;
  non-revival includes descriptor reappearance and reference non-reuse.
- The next native investigation has a precise discriminator and controls;
  other k75 obligations remain live in named design leaves. No adoption,
  implementation, new public schema or parent completion is inferred.

## Decisions (running log)

- Keep observation, reference lifetime and dispatch ordering in the native
  adapter's serialized owner. Distinguish irreversible known-closure retirement
  from withdrawal caused by observation loss or capture end. The latter is a
  proposed availability policy, not a new human agreement or closure fact.
- Publication needs an evidenced registration barrier, not register-and-read
  by assumption. Delayed native events need window/registration lifetime
  attribution independently of sender authentication. An inability to establish
  that premise must be surfaced before adoption, not hidden behind a protocol
  field or a model assumption.
- Queue insertion is not send initiation. The final check and actual initiation
  are ordered with retirement; reply waiting does not block the owner. Earlier
  possible sends remain uncertain. Separate request cancellation from capture
  observation ownership so cancellation cannot silently stop closure tracking.
- Present a concrete proposal for review before investigating its native
  feasibility. No new agreement is sought until evidence makes the availability,
  lifecycle and complete protocol trade-offs concrete. All inherited agreement
  requirements remain with the final contract child.
