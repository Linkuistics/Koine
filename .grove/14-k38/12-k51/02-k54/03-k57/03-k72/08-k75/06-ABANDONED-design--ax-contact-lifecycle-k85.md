# ax-contact-lifecycle-k85


## Goal

Determine the duration/release condition of AX-contact inhibition sufficiently
to present its lifecycle consequence for explicit agreement.



## Context

Consume the matched-control report and survey Lead 2. A single successful AX
read inhibited automatic termination in the recorded window; exiting the reader
and destroying its owned client receive right did not recover eligibility.
Those observations do not establish permanence. The k104 public-AX choice does not authorize keeping targets alive indefinitely.
Focus this evidence on public AX contact; no private transport work is required.

## Done when

- Inspect the loaded AX/AppKit path for the inhibition and paired release
  mechanism on exact platform bytes in a VM, without attachment, injection or
  target modification as a product dependency. Choose the next release schedule
  from that evidence, not an open-ended series of guesses.
- Use matched no-AX, one-read, and evidenced release controls with independent
  process/TAL witnesses. Keep duration observed, mechanism inferred and missing
  schedules distinct. Preserve real program exit records and frozen inputs.
- Where reachable, exercise admitted capture, release, automatic termination,
  restoration and refusal of ended capture identities and old references; do not credit
  nontermination as restoration. If unreachable or unproved, state why and what
  remains unknown. Excluded downstream effects are not reopened as guarantees.
- Obtain explicit agreement on the concrete lifecycle consequence, or preserve
  the conflict before adoption. No effect-boundary agreement can substitute for
  this decision. Carry evidence and accepted costs into the current spec and
  k87's full protocol/handoff.

## Notes

This is the named retained lifecycle obligation, not a new general survey or
permission to run abandoned activation experiments. A fixture result remains
bounded; distinguish any unmodified-application evidence separately.
