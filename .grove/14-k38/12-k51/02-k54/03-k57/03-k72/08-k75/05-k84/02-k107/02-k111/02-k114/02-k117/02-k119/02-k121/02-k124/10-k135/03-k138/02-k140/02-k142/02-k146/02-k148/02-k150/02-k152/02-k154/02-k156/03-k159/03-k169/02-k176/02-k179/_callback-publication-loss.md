## Decisions (running log)

- Separate the publication observation contract from its executable integration.
  The native recorder already places send before visibility, while Trace joins
  send directly to receipt and the frozen producer reports assume that edge.
  Adding syscall results cannot repair receiver feasibility by itself: the new
  reader must preserve an attempted write's causal edge without claiming its
  return, and keep every independent legacy diagnostic honest. First specify
  exact bytes, observations, pending states and discriminating counterexamples;
  a fresh design child implements/falsifies that contract at the existing Trace
  seam and renews the full corpus. Every original Done when stays binding on
  this node. The first child neither changes executable version semantics nor
  establishes transport loss or native readiness.
