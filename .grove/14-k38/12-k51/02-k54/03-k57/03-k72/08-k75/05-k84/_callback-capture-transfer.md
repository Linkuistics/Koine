## Goal

Establish a public-client callback capture and actual native-right transfer
construction with attributed fresh sampling and continuous ownership.



## Context

earlier evidence without assuming it solves capture. k104 selected public AX
for target operations; the capture/right-transfer channel is a separate obligation
and retains hard resource admission before Koine acquires transferred resources. The foreground
when the callback executes is agreed; original-event targeting and server-side
foreground selection are not required alternatives to re-ask.
