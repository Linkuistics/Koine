## Goal

Compose the evidenced capture and bounded native transport into a complete
resident-Koine transfer candidate and k87 agreement handoff.



## Context

Consume callback-sample-witness-k107 and capture-transfer-admission-k108 from
their evidence, not their completion marks. `docs/verification/callback-capture-transfer.md`
contains the boundary/ownership ledger and unresolved packaging alternatives.

## Done when

- Specify and evidence discovery and authentication of the actual resident
  process before disclosing a grant; a bootstrap name, file, PID or nonce alone
  is not server authentication. Authenticate peer attribution without restricting
  bearer credentials to one client or introducing an unagreed signer allowlist.
- Resolve public service registration, installation/login launch and one
  resident-process ownership. A launchd declaration is a packaging question,
  not permission for an extra helper, second UI/server instance, or consent
  owner. Take any necessary composition change to the human concretely.
- Specify actual transfer, request correlation, continuous sender/runtime/
  receiver ownership and the acknowledgment that permits sender release.
  Exercise the selected transport and authenticated resident path, not merely a
  numeric-port fixture. Never reacquire by PID to repair a failed transfer.
- Order live grant and resource reservation with transfer adoption/revocation
  at Koine's existing authority boundary; old authorization cannot admit new
  captures. Grant admission for later GraphQL/native work remains separate.
- Specify and witness cancellation/disconnect before and after possible send,
  rejection, partial failure, missing acknowledgment, duplicate traffic, client
  crash and Koine restart. Preserve unknown outcomes after a possible send and
  no automatic mutation replay. Define orphan disposal, finite reservations,
  retained rights, concurrency/churn limits, waits and cleanup ownership.
- Propose capture ownership after acknowledgment, client disconnect and Koine
  restart explicitly for k87. A fresh transfer of an actually retained right is
  distinct from rebinding an old reference or silently sampling a new foreground
  process. Do not adopt final reference grammar or restart survival here.
- Reconcile the machine spec's candidate description, capture/client guidance
  and views. Give k87 concrete product budgets, native evidence limits, all
  remaining human decisions and the k52 capture/IPC migration instructions.
  Check every original k84 Done when against the delivered evidence; add any
  missing named work before closure. Preserve the k75/k87 whole-protocol review
  and agreement before implementation.
