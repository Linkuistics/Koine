replay through one fixed Trace, without importing legacy message shortcuts.

executable successor and all original ancestor criteria. Read the four reviewed

## Decisions (running log)

- Use one physical Trace and a read-only original-coordinate producer view.
  Existing combined wrappers build legacy edges or mutate witness edges; they
  remain compatibility adapters, not the successor's causal authority. Pure
  predicate extraction must preserve all old report bytes and failure precedence.
- Keep local validated receipt/payload decisions separate from sender-observation
  and cross-hop truth. Invalid local bytes confer no R, but cannot suppress later
  independent raw source/right/loop evidence. Graph construction precedes all
  dependent checks and retains every individually supported edge under U/I.
- Enumerate non-message witnesses before the single SCC pass. Per-claim support
  includes every necessary contributor plus a canonical acyclic witness for
  each required order; an alternate path cannot remove a separately necessary
  cyclic edge. The focused review must falsify both-path and multi-contributor
  cases before this proposed policy is implemented.
- Commission callback-publication-replay-k206 ahead of planning k205. Source
  predicate extraction and support policy merit fresh-context design review.
  K205 retains complete executable delivery planning; neither design nor plan
  closes any original k203 criterion. No product ADR or glossary change follows.
