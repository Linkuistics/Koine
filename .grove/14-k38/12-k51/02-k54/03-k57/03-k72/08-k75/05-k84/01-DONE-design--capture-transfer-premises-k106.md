## Decisions (running log)

- Separate candidate discovery from the definitive sample: a PID or launch
  notification may nominate a right to acquire, but cannot date a later sample.
  Keep pre-held candidate rights as Lead 3's fallback; compare a bounded
  acquire-then-resample alternative without selecting either as feasible.
- Use the existing native VM seam to establish the platform premises. A model
  that assumes a fresh sample or bounded native receive would answer neither
  uncertainty. The full protocol remains subject to k87's agreement and review.
- The bounded adversarial read found no supported material gap in the
  conditional assessment or the k107–k109 obligation map. That is a contract
  review only; it adds no native feasibility or runtime guarantee. No transport,
  packaging or restart policy is adopted by this increment.
