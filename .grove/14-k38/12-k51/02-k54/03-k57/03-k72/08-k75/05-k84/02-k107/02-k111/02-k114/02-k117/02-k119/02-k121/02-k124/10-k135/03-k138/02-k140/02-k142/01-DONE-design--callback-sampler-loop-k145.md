  setup/preheld acquisition. Its measured baseline is restored at the matching
  return. Ordinary post-return work runs before host_end; after-callback publishes
  callback-returned and ends the host callout before later socket delivery.
- V8 adds local loop observations and a separate result column. Existing Trace,
  controller and candidate reports remain independent. A local timeout cannot
  downgrade controller malformation. Incomplete sampler prefixes are not bridged.

  acceptance-only vocabulary. K146 retains that complete producer work rather
  than interpreting the local cancellation examples as composed controller aborts.
- Executable mutation controls test this local ordering claim; k144 is already
  chartered to commission fresh adversarial review of the completed producer.
  No additional in-session reviewer duplicates that incomplete-composition read.
