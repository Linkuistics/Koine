  through `parse_record_lines` rather than duplicating its validation grammar.

  history truth, supported edges, F/U/I and lifecycle belong to k203.
- Keep storage format versioning separate from analyzer versions. V11–V25 are
  the only bases; V26 retains its original wrapper. No native or product change.
- A finite actual-file grammar and literal bounds need direct file tests, not a
  separate state model. Broader replay model choices stay with k203.
