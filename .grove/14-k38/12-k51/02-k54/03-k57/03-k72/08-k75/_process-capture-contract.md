## Decisions (running log)

- This work exceeds one focused session: authenticated asynchronous observation
  has no adopted native mechanism, independently of capture/transfer, lifecycle
  and consent. Decompose without weakening any Done when. The first child
  specifies observation admission, irreversible retirement and ordering with
  sends, with an explicit native discriminator; later design work must supply
  the evidence before the complete protocol can be agreed. A conditional state
  contract is not native feasibility or permission for k52 to start.
