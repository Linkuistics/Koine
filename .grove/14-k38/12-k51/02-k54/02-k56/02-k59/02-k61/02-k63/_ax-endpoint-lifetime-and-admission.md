recommendation before k57 can settle a contract.

## Notes
Use TestAnyware for all native/GUI execution; host builds only. Preserve before
and after hashes of every measurement input. TestAnyware can report a false
timeout for synchronous app execution: redirect guest stdout/stderr and record
the program's exit status in its own completion file; a launcher is not proof.
The existing public and private wrapper counterexamples must not be reintroduced
as fallbacks. One OS-build match plus internal symbols is a diagnostic guard,
not an agreed compatibility policy. k57 still owns the human agreement on
capture/transfer/reference/restart/prepublication framing.

## Decisions (running log)

- Inspect a real read-only AX request as the admission exchange, with a fresh
  reply right and audited reply followed by a retained-task live read. The
  unknown-ID diagnostic cannot become a product no-op by assumption. Native
  execution stays in disposable TestAnyware clone `koine-k63-admission`.
- Spend this leaf's single reviewer on the receiver ownership and forwarding
  argument against primary platform evidence, independently of the admission
  experiment. The claim to challenge is that an admitted send right continues
  to address only the captured process through every later effect; audit
  equality alone cannot establish that claim.
- The frozen signed/hardened diagnostic admitted an actual `AXRole` read,
  rejected a wrong retained task, and completed all direct effects at PID 804.
  Separate death-before-effect and death-between-effect schedules refused all
  later primitives. The same direct primitives also refused after actual PID
  820 reuse (99,408 allocations); the independent successor witness was
  unchanged. These are bounded Cocoa observations, not receiver immobility.
- Split at the verified admission/death boundary. Receiver registration and
  exec/restoration need a different native instrument; policy attribution and
  adoption costs need a separately launched consent experiment. Preserve both
  as design children, with the original feasibility criteria still binding.

## Decomposition

1. `ax-audited-admission-and-death-k64`: preserve the read-only exchange,
   same-endpoint effects, controls, actual PID reuse, and scoped design evidence.
2. `ax-receiver-lifetime-k65`: establish the actual registered receive-right lifecycle,
   top-level routing, exec and automatic restoration, or a precise conflict.
3. `ax-endpoint-policy-and-adoption-k66`: establish denial/consent and maintenance costs,
   then synthesize the full feasibility result for k57 or the human.

Each child is independently verifiable. These are distinct experiments, not a
declared repetition series. No additional review or implementation stage is cut.
