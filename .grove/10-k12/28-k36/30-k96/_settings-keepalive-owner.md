## Accepted contract

One serialized owner governs ticks, ordinary commands, validation, publication,
failure and cleanup in one lock/latch/ordinal domain. Only logged UI-independent
waits and deferred ticks allowed by the agreement change elapsed timing; preserve
input order, sleeps/polls and one attempt. Required capture validation and first
failure precede later input. A planned cooperative worker stop has a separate
matched lifecycle record and confirmed exit, and cannot erase earlier failures.
Unexpected exit, interrupted required capture or missing stop evidence remains
unavailable. Retain finite enforceable complete originals and partials, with
protected final/cleanup capacity; unqualified holder/storage boundaries hold.
